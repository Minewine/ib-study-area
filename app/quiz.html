Quiz — practice sessions

Set up a session

Pick a subject, topic and format. Sessions run 20 questions (10 for a quick pass): MCQs are marked instantly; written answers are self-marked against the markscheme, and the summary flags weak topics with study advice. Before each answer is revealed you say whether you are sure or guessing, so the summary can show where you felt confident but were wrong.

Chemistry HL also has Paper 1A-style calculator-free practice (multiple choice, no calculator) and Paper 1B-style data practice (short data-based questions). These are original practice questions written for the 2025 syllabus. They are not IB past-paper questions and do not predict what will be on the exam.

Topics & teaching status

Topics are suggested as “taught” once every syllabus code they cover has finished in the class schedule. Your own choice — not yet taught, learning, or ready to revise — always wins and syncs across devices.
