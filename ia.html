Internal Assessment & Extended Essay

DP1 rule: collect, don't write

Keep an idea log — every lab observation, curiosity or real-world question noted in 2 minutes. It becomes the IA/exploration/EE shortlist in DP2. Never start writing before the school timeline says so.

Sciences IA — scientific investigation (Bio/Chem HL, 20%)

10 hours, 24 marks, report ≤ 3000 words. Four equally-weighted criteria: research design · data analysis · conclusion · evaluation (6 marks each). At ISL: the criteria are practised in DP1 (hydrogen peroxide in Nov, beetroot/osmosis in Jan — debriefs below), experiments late June, draft early Sept of DP2, final early Nov — per the course outline. Calibrate against the official TSM marked samples (via school) — and read exemplars for the quality bar only, never to imitate: IB similarity screening is strict.

Practice IA debriefs — the criteria in the wild (fill within a day of each session)

The DP1 practice sessions exist so the real IA (experiments late June) feels familiar. Right after each session, capture what each criterion actually looked like: where the marks were won, where they went, and which method step caused it. Short honest bullets beat essays — they feed straight into the June write-ups.

November · hydrogen peroxide (all four criteria practised)

CriterionWhat to capture (2–3 lines each)
Research designWas the research question measurable and specific? Which variables were identified and controlled — and where did the method lose precision (range, repeats, confounders)?
Data analysisHow were raw data processed (means, uncertainties, graphs)? Did the processing reveal the trend or smooth it away? What did outliers say about the method?
ConclusionDid the conclusion answer the research question using the data, with the trend stated at the right strength? Where did expectation and observation diverge?
EvaluationWhich specific limitation moved the result most — and was the proposed improvement realistic and tied to the data, not generic?

January · beetroot membranes + osmosis (the pair splits the criteria)

Beetroot is the research-design rehearsal (variables, controls, dilution series); osmosis rehearses analysis → conclusion → evaluation (processing, trend, limitations).

Text saves as you type and syncs when logged in (key ia-debriefs). The official criterion wording lives in the guide/TSM — this page is the practice view, not the rubric.

Math exploration (20%)

Choose something you can genuinely explore, not an impressive topic. Criteria and marked samples: official Assessed Student Work 2020 · Math AA TSM 2023. Idea sources: IB Maths Resources.

History investigation (30% at SL!)

20 hours, 24 marks, max 7 sources, no reflection section under the new guide. A third of the history grade — treat it like a mini-thesis. Start source-gathering early DP2.

EE (new guide)

Subject-focused or interdisciplinary pathway; 5 criteria / 30 marks; 4000 words; reflection form ≤ 500 words across three sessions. Read the official EE guide (FA2027) — old-guide criteria found anywhere else are obsolete.
