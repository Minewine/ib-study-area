Guide: Feb 2023, first assessment May 2025 — only May 2025, Nov 2025, May 2026 papers match. Everything older in any archive is the dead 2016 syllabus: different structure, different IA.

Assessment

ComponentWeighting
Paper 1 — 2h, 75 marks: 1A (40 MCQ) + 1B (data-based questions)36%
Paper 2 — 2h30, 80 marks: Section A data/short answer, Section B extended response44%
IA — scientific investigation (10 h, 24 marks, report ≤ 3000 words)20%

Paper 1B (data handling) is the new skill: read graphs, design tables, evaluate methods. No data booklet exists for biology — the skills must be automatic.
