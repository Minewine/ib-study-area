Command terms that decide marks

TermWhat the examiner wants
Stateone word/short phrase, no reasoning
Identifypick out from context
Defineexact meaning
Outlinebrief structure, little detail
Describegive a detailed account
Distinguishdifferences between two things
Explaingive reasons/causes — the 'why'
Calculateshow method, units, significant figures
Determineusually needs data extraction + calculation
Predictgive an expected result with justification
Sketchcorrect shape/labelled graph, not to scale
Drawprecise labelled diagram
Compareboth sides with explicit links
Discussbalanced review with range of arguments
Evaluatestrengths AND weaknesses, weighed judgement
Justifygive valid reasons with evidence
To what extentweigh the evidence, then conclude

Matching the verb to the answer shape is the cheapest marks in bio/chem/history.
