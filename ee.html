Reflection sessions (new guide)

Three sessions with the supervisor, culminating in the viva voce, written up on the reflection form (≤ 500 words total). The form rewards process — decisions, setbacks, changes of direction and what they taught — not a summary of the essay's content. Keep dated RRS notes as each session happens; reconstructing them later reads exactly like what it is.
